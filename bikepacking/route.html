Destination

We didn't have a predefined destination: we were just enjoying the journey, wherever it would take us.

Our vague idea was to ride our bikes from Europe toward Central Asia to cross the Pamirs and maybe reach Pakistan and India. In the end, we headed to Mongolia instead because the Pakistani-Indian border was closed, and from there we improvised depending on what we felt might be more interesting to us. Total freedom!
